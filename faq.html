Questions

Why do servers keep moving to HTTP/3 and QUIC?

To reduce latency and improve reliability, especially on mobile and high-latency networks. QUIC uses UDP with features like connection migration and better loss recovery.

What are the biggest ongoing challenges?

Security (misconfigurations, unpatched CVEs, DDoS), scaling for unpredictable spikes, and the rapid pace of protocol/standards change that requires continuous learning.

How do edge computing and CDNs change server design?

They push content and logic closer to users, cutting round-trip time and absorbing surges; origin servers focus more on dynamic and stateful work.

Where does serverless fit with web servers?

Serverless can offload some dynamic tasks and autoscale without managing machines; many stacks combine reverse proxies, origins, and serverless functions.

What future trends should admins plan for?

Broader HTTP/3 adoption, AI-assisted ops (predictive scaling, anomaly detection), zero-trust security, quantum-resistant crypto, and greener, energy-efficient hosting.
